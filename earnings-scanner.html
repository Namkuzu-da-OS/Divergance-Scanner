📊 PREM Score Breakdown

The PREM (Pre-Earnings Momentum) score evaluates stocks 5-10 days before earnings to find optimal entry points for earnings plays.

📈 RSI Position 5-15 pts
• RSI 40-70 (healthy momentum): +15 pts
• RSI < 30 (momentum reset bounce): +10 pts
• RSI > 70 (high momentum, less upside): +5 pts
📊 Trend Alignment 5-10 pts
• Strong uptrend (bullish momentum): +10 pts
• Uptrend or neutral: +7 pts
• Downtrend (contrarian setup): +5 pts
💰 Options Flow 0-15 pts
• Strong bullish flow (net call delta): +15 pts
• Moderate bullish flow: +10 pts
• Neutral or mixed flow: +5 pts
• No flow data: +0 pts
📉 IV Rank 5-15 pts
• Low IV (room to expand into earnings): +15 pts
• Moderate IV: +10 pts
• High IV (already priced in): +5 pts
🌍 Market Context 5-15 pts
• SPY bullish + low VIX: +15 pts
• SPY neutral or moderate VIX: +10 pts
• SPY bearish or high VIX: +5 pts
⏱️ Timing 5-10 pts
• 6-8 days to earnings (optimal): +10 pts
• 5 or 9-10 days: +7 pts
• Outside PREM window: +5 pts
🌙 Earnings Time 0-5 pts
• AMC (After Market Close): +5 pts - preferred for overnight plays
• BMO (Before Market Open): +3 pts
• Unknown: +0 pts

Score Tiers

HIGH CONVICTION 80+ points - Strong setup, multiple factors aligned
TRADEABLE 70-79 points - Good setup, worth considering
WATCH <70 points - Monitor but wait for better setup

Max possible score: ~85 pts. Scores are relative - use for comparison between candidates.
