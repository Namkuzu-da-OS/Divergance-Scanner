📊 PREM Score Breakdown

The PREM (Pre-Earnings Momentum) score evaluates stocks 5-10 days before earnings to find optimal entry points for earnings plays.

📈 RSI Position 5-15 pts
• RSI 40-70 (healthy momentum): +15 pts
• RSI < 30 (momentum reset bounce): +10 pts
• RSI > 70 (high momentum, less upside): +5 pts
📊 Trend Alignment 5-10 pts
• Strong uptrend (bullish momentum): +10 pts
• Uptrend or neutral: +7 pts
• Downtrend (contrarian setup): +5 pts
💰 Options Flow 0-15 pts
• Strong bullish flow (net call delta): +15 pts
• Moderate bullish flow: +10 pts
• Neutral or mixed flow: +5 pts
• No flow data: +0 pts
📉 IV Rank 5-15 pts
• Low IV (room to expand into earnings): +15 pts
• Moderate IV: +10 pts
• High IV (already priced in): +5 pts
🌍 Market Context 5-15 pts
• SPY bullish + low VIX: +15 pts
• SPY neutral or moderate VIX: +10 pts
• SPY bearish or high VIX: +5 pts
⏱️ Timing 5-10 pts
• 6-8 days to earnings (optimal): +10 pts
• 5 or 9-10 days: +7 pts
• Outside PREM window: +5 pts
🌙 Earnings Time 0-5 pts
• AMC (After Market Close): +5 pts - preferred for overnight plays
• BMO (Before Market Open): +3 pts
• Unknown: +0 pts

Score Tiers

HIGH CONVICTION 80+ points - Strong setup, multiple factors aligned
TRADEABLE 70-79 points - Good setup, worth considering
WATCH <70 points - Monitor but wait for better setup

Max possible score: ~85 pts. Scores are relative - use for comparison between candidates.

📖 HOW THE EARNINGS SCANNER WORKS Reference

WHAT THIS DASHBOARD SHOWS

The Earnings Scanner tracks stocks with upcoming earnings events and analyzes their options activity leading into the announcement. Use this to identify pre-earnings setups, avoid holding positions through earnings, or find premium-selling opportunities.

DATA SOURCE

Source: SQLite earnings_scans/earnings_results

Scanner: monitor/earnings-scanner.js

Update Frequency: Every 5 minutes

KEY EARNINGS CONCEPTS

IV Crush Implied volatility drops 30-50% immediately after earnings. Options lose value even if stock moves in your direction. This is why holding options through earnings is risky.
IV Rank Where current IV sits vs. the past year (0-100%). Typically spikes before earnings. High IV Rank = expensive options, good for selling premium.
Expected Move What the options market implies the stock will move post-earnings. Compare to historical moves to find edge.
AMC / BMO After Market Close / Before Market Open - when earnings are announced

EARNINGS STRATEGIES

Pre-Earnings Run Buy calls/puts BEFORE earnings when IV is lower, sell before announcement to avoid IV crush
Premium Selling Sell strangles/iron condors when IV Rank is high (>50%), profit from IV crush even if stock moves
Post-Earnings Move Wait for earnings reaction, then trade the follow-through move (gap and go, or fade the gap)

WHAT TO WATCH

  • Days to Earnings: 0-3 days = highest IV, watch for premium selling setups
  • IV Rank > 50%: Options are expensive, consider selling premium
  • Historical Moves: Does the stock typically beat/miss? How much does it move?
  • Sector Peers: If similar companies already reported, what was the reaction?

RISK WARNING

⚠️ Holding options through earnings is gambling, not trading. IV crush can wipe out your position even if you're right on direction. Either close before earnings or use defined-risk strategies (spreads).
