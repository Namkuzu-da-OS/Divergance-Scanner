📖 HOW THE GAPS SCANNER WORKS Reference ▼

WHAT THIS DASHBOARD SHOWS

The Gaps Scanner dynamically discovers stocks gapping 2%+ from their previous close. It scans the entire S&P 500 and NASDAQ to find opportunities - NOT a fixed watchlist. Best used during pre-market (6:00 AM - 9:30 AM ET).

DATA SOURCE

File: data/premarket.json

Scanner: monitor/premarket-scanner.js

Update Frequency: Every 5 minutes

DYNAMIC DISCOVERY SOURCES

The scanner queries multiple APIs to find what's moving:

volume_leader Top 15 volume stocks from S&P 500 - /api/movers/$SPX?sort=VOLUME
gainer Stocks up 2%+ today - /api/movers/$SPX?sort=PERCENT_CHANGE_UP
loser Stocks down 2%+ today - /api/movers/$SPX?sort=PERCENT_CHANGE_DOWN
nasdaq_mover Top NASDAQ volume - /api/movers/$COMPX?sort=VOLUME
watchlist Your personal watchlist from data/watchlist.json
core Always checked: SPY, QQQ, IWM, DIA

GAP CLASSIFICATION

HUGE Gap ≥ 5% - Major catalyst, high volatility
LARGE Gap 3-5% - Significant move
MODERATE Gap 2-3% - Standard gap trade candidate
SMALL/FLAT Gap < 2% - Filtered out (below threshold)

SCORING (0-100)

Gap Size (40 pts) ≥5% = 40, ≥3% = 30, ≥2% = 20, ≥1% = 10
Pre-Market Volume (20 pts) >1M = 20, >500K = 15, >100K = 10
Catalyst (20 pts) Earnings = 20, News = 15
Futures Aligned (20 pts) Gap direction matches SPY/QQQ = 20

TIER MEANINGS

HIGH_CONVICTION Score ≥ 70 - Strong gap with multiple factors aligned
TRADEABLE Score 50-69 - Good gap setup
WATCH Score 30-49 - Monitor but wait for confirmation

🎯 GAP AND GO vs GAP AND FADE

The critical question: Will this gap continue (GO) or reverse (FADE)? Use these factors to decide.

🔴 GAP AND FADE (Short Candidates)

Gaps that are likely to fill/reverse:

Parabolic Move 5-day momentum > 20% = unsustainable, mean reversion likely
RSI > 80 Extremely overbought, exhaustion signal
BB Position > 1.0 Price ABOVE upper Bollinger Band = stretched, will snap back
Above Call Wall Price blew through options structure = no gamma support above
Above Expected Move Price exceeded options-implied range = overextended
Far Above Max Pain $5+ above max pain = gravitational pull back down
High Volatility HV > 100% = wild swings, hard to hold direction

🟢 GAP AND GO (Long Candidates)

Gaps that are likely to continue:

Orderly Trend 5-day momentum < 5% = steady accumulation, not panic buying
RSI 60-75 Strong but not extreme, room to run
BB Position 0.6-0.9 Near upper band but WITHIN = trending, not overextended
Above Gamma Flip Dealers hedging calls by buying stock = self-reinforcing buying
Call Wall Nearby Heavy call OI just above = magnet pulling price up
Within Expected Move Price inside options-implied range = "normal" move
Moderate Volatility HV 30-60% = controlled trend, easier to hold

📊 QUICK REFERENCE COMPARISON

Factor FADE GO
5-Day Momentum > 20% (parabolic) < 5% (orderly)
RSI > 80 60-75
BB Position > 1.0 (outside) 0.6-0.9 (within)
vs Call Wall Above (no support) At/near (magnet)
vs Gamma Flip Way above Just above
vs Expected Move Beyond upper Within range
Historical Vol > 100% 30-60%

💡 KEY INSIGHT: When price blows through ALL options structure (above call wall, above expected move, above gamma flip) with parabolic momentum = FADE. When price breaks above gamma flip with orderly momentum and call wall support nearby = GO.
