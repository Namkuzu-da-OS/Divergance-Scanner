📖 HOW THE GAPS SCANNER WORKS Reference

WHAT THIS DASHBOARD SHOWS

The Gaps Scanner dynamically discovers stocks gapping 2%+ from their previous close. It scans the entire S&P 500 and NASDAQ to find opportunities - NOT a fixed watchlist. Best used during pre-market (6:00 AM - 9:30 AM ET).

DATA SOURCE

File: data/premarket.json

Scanner: monitor/premarket-scanner.js

Update Frequency: Every 5 minutes

DYNAMIC DISCOVERY SOURCES

The scanner queries multiple APIs to find what's moving:

volume_leader Top 15 volume stocks from S&P 500 - /api/movers/$SPX?sort=VOLUME
gainer Stocks up 2%+ today - /api/movers/$SPX?sort=PERCENT_CHANGE_UP
loser Stocks down 2%+ today - /api/movers/$SPX?sort=PERCENT_CHANGE_DOWN
nasdaq_mover Top NASDAQ volume - /api/movers/$COMPX?sort=VOLUME
watchlist Your personal watchlist from data/watchlist.json
core Always checked: SPY, QQQ, IWM, DIA

GAP CLASSIFICATION

HUGE Gap ≥ 5% - Major catalyst, high volatility
LARGE Gap 3-5% - Significant move
MODERATE Gap 2-3% - Standard gap trade candidate
SMALL/FLAT Gap < 2% - Filtered out (below threshold)

SCORING (0-100)

Gap Size (40 pts) ≥5% = 40, ≥3% = 30, ≥2% = 20, ≥1% = 10
Pre-Market Volume (20 pts) >1M = 20, >500K = 15, >100K = 10
Catalyst (20 pts) Earnings = 20, News = 15
Futures Aligned (20 pts) Gap direction matches SPY/QQQ = 20

TIER MEANINGS

HIGH_CONVICTION Score ≥ 70 - Strong gap with multiple factors aligned
TRADEABLE Score 50-69 - Good gap setup
WATCH Score 30-49 - Monitor but wait for confirmation

CONTROL API (Port 8084)

POST http://localhost:8084/pause       # Pause scanner
POST http://localhost:8084/resume      # Resume scanner
POST http://localhost:8084/scan        # Trigger immediate scan
GET  http://localhost:8084/status      # Scanner status
GET  http://localhost:8084/latest      # Latest scan data
GET  http://localhost:8084/today       # Today's stats
