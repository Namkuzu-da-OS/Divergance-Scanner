📖 HOW THE ZONE SCANNER WORKS Reference

WHAT THIS DASHBOARD SHOWS

The Zone Scanner displays results from the Bloodhound Scanner - an autonomous opportunity detection system that finds high-confluence trading setups. It scans every 2 minutes and identifies where price sits relative to gamma walls (support/resistance from options positioning).

DATA SOURCE

Source: SQLite via /api/scan/latest

Scanner: monitor/bloodhound-scanner.js

Update Frequency: Every 2 minutes

ZONE TYPES

BUY_ZONE Price at or near the PUT wall (support). Bullish setup - consider longs.
SELL_ZONE Price at or near the CALL wall (resistance). Bearish setup - consider shorts.
PINNED Price trapped between call and put walls. Expect range-bound movement.
BREAKOUT Price above call wall. Momentum up - can run.
BREAKDOWN Price below put wall. Momentum down - can fall further.

CONFLUENCE SCORE (0-80)

Each symbol gets a score based on multiple factors aligning:

Technical (25 pts) RSI oversold/overbought, Bollinger Band position, trend alignment
Levels (25 pts) At gamma walls, VWAP, confluence zones, breakout/breakdown
Volume (15 pts) Volume spike (2x+), elevated volume (1.5x+)
Context (15 pts) Aligned with SPY trend, market regime

HISTORY BADGES

🆕 NEW First time this symbol appeared at this zone
📈 Day 2 Second consecutive day at same zone - higher conviction
🔥 Streak 3+ days at same zone - very high conviction setup

TIER MEANINGS

HIGH_CONVICTION Score ≥ 56 + at wall (0.5%), OR Score ≥ 64 + near wall (1.5%)
TRADEABLE Score ≥ 48 + at wall + trend-aligned
WATCH Interesting but not actionable yet
