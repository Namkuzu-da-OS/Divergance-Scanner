📖 HOW THE OPTIONS FLOW SCANNER WORKS Reference

WHAT THIS DASHBOARD SHOWS

The Options Flow Scanner detects unusual options activity that may signal institutional positioning. It focuses purely on options-specific data: volume/OI ratios, premium flow, IV rank, and call/put imbalances. Note: Options don't trade pre-market (6-9:30 AM ET) - this scanner is most useful during market hours.

DATA SOURCE

Source: /api/opportunities/latest

Scanner: monitor/opportunity-scanner.js

Update Frequency: Every 5 minutes

KEY METRICS EXPLAINED

IV Rank Where current implied volatility sits vs. the last year (0-100%). High IV = expensive options, good for selling. Low IV = cheap options, good for buying.
Vol/OI Ratio Today's options volume vs. open interest. Ratio > 1.0 = unusual activity (more contracts traded today than existed). Can signal big moves coming.
C/P Ratio Call volume divided by put volume. > 1.0 = bullish bias, < 1.0 = bearish bias. Extreme values (2x+) signal strong directional bets.
Net Premium Net dollar flow into calls vs. puts. Positive = more $ going into calls (bullish). Negative = more $ going into puts (bearish).

WHAT WAS REMOVED

This scanner previously detected gaps and volume spikes, but those signals overlapped with the GAPS scanner. Now it focuses purely on options flow data to avoid duplication.

SIGNAL TYPES

IV_HIGH IV Rank > 70% - Options are expensive, consider selling premium
IV_LOW IV Rank < 30% - Options are cheap, consider buying
UNUSUAL_VOL Vol/OI ratio unusually high - big positioning happening
CALL_HEAVY C/P ratio > 2.0 - Strong bullish options bias
PUT_HEAVY C/P ratio < 0.5 - Strong bearish options bias

DYNAMIC DISCOVERY

Like the Gaps scanner, this scanner dynamically discovers symbols from S&P 500 movers, NASDAQ movers, and 52-week extremes. It's not limited to a fixed watchlist.

CONTROL API (Port 8083)

POST http://localhost:8083/pause       # Pause scanner
POST http://localhost:8083/resume      # Resume scanner
POST http://localhost:8083/scan        # Trigger immediate scan
GET  http://localhost:8083/status      # Scanner status
