## THE FIVE CORE RULES

### RULE 1: Higher Timeframe Context for All Scalps (MANDATORY)

**Statement:** Every scalp trade MUST correlate with higher timeframe context (Daily/Weekly levels).

**What This Means:**
- Cannot execute scalps on 1/5/15-min charts in isolation
- Before every scalp, identify: "Which daily/weekly level am I near?"
- Scalp entries have ZERO edge without higher timeframe support

**Examples of Valid Context:**
- Scalping near Daily Open (DO) = valid context
- Scalping near Weekly Midpoint (WM) = valid context
- Scalping while Daily High (PDH) is 2% away = still valid (approaching level)
- Scalping at Weekly Low (WL) with reversal = ultra-high probability

**Examples of Invalid Context:**
- "I just like the 5-min chart right now" = INVALID, no context
- Entry 50% away from any daily/weekly level = INVALID
- Scalping in the middle of daily range with no confluence = INVALID

**Wingman's Authority:** CHALLENGE any scalp without stated higher timeframe context

### RULE 2: 1% Risk Maximum (HARD STOP)

**Statement:** No trade may risk more than 1% of account ($200) under any circumstances.

**What This Means:**
- Position size = Risk / (Entry - Stop)
- Maximum risk per trade: $200
- If you need to size larger, the trade isn't worth it
- No exceptions, no negotiations

**Formula:**
```
Position Size = $200 / (Entry Price - Stop Price)

If result > available capital, REDUCE POSITION SIZE, don't increase stop
```

**Wingman's Authority:** REJECT any trade that violates this rule

### RULE 3: Daily Loss Limit = $500 (HARD STOP)

**Statement:** If account loses $500 in a single day, ALL TRADING STOPS immediately.

**What This Means:**
- Once -$500 realized, no more trades for rest of day
- Even if you feel "now I'll get it back" - NO
- This is discipline enforcement, period
- Return tomorrow with clear mind

**Tracking:**

- Dashboard shows "Daily Risk Used" progress bar
- RED alert at $400 (approaching limit)
- STOP all activity at $500

**Recovery Protocol:**
- Step away from trading for remainder of day
- Journal what happened (emotions, decisions)
- Review with Wingman before next session
- Do NOT revenge trade

**Wingman's Authority:** FORCE STOP all trading once -$500 limit hit

### RULE 4: Weekly Loss Limit = $1,000 (HARD STOP)

**What This Means:**
- Once -$1,000 cumulative, STOP for 5 trading days
- Reassess plan, market conditions, and psychology
- Return with reduced risk (0.5% instead of 1%)
- Full review required

**Tracking:**

- HARD STOP at $1,000

**Recovery Protocol:**

- Reduce position size by 50% when returning
- Do NOT try to "make it back quickly"

**Wingman's Authority:** FORCE STOP all trading once -$1,000 limit hit, reduce risk to 0.5% for next week

### RULE 5: Fibonacci 127% Extension = Primary Exit Target

**Statement:** 127% Fibonacci extension is the go-to exit on MOST setups (not all, but most).

**What This Means:**
- When taking a scalp with range entry: target 127% of range move
- When taking VWAP reversion: can extend to 127% beyond VWAP
- When range breakout: next likely reversal point is 127% extension
- This is the HIGH PROBABILITY ZONE, not guaranteed but reliable

**Why 127%?**
- Market moves in waves/fractals
- After 100% extension, 127% is natural next resistance
- Historical data shows high reversal rate at 127%
- Part of Fibonacci sequence applied to trading ranges

**Important Exception:**
- If trade is profitable enough before 127%, take profit early
- But 127% should be TARGET, not exit early out of fear
- Be patient and let 127% become the profit-taking zone

**Wingman's Authority:** QUESTION exits taken before 127% if still room to target

## SUPPORTING RULES (Mandatory But Not Absolute)

### Rule S-1: Respect All Stop Orders
- Stop is set BEFORE entry, never moved wider
- When stop is hit, exit IMMEDIATELY
- Exception: Move stop into profit only

### Rule S-2: Volume Confirmation Required
- No entry without above-average volume
- Breakouts require volume spike
- Reversions need volume confirmation on re-entry
- Low volume = lower probability = skip setup

### Rule S-3: No Trading First/Last 15 Minutes
- First 15 min: Let market settle, find VWAP anchor
- Last 15 min: Avoid surprise reversals, close out positions
- Exception: Closing losses if daily limit approaching

### Rule S-4: No Revenge Trading
- After loss, wait minimum 3 bars before next entry
- After loss streak (3+), reduce size by 50%
- Emotions must cool before next trade
- Wingman will call you out

### Rule S-5: Pre-Trade Planning (Mandatory)
- Before ANY entry, know:
  - Entry price (exact)
  - Stop loss price (exact)
  - Target price (exact)
  - Position size (calculated, not guessed)
  - Why this trade (which strategy, what context)
- If you can't answer all 5, DO NOT ENTER

### Rule S-6: No News Trades
- Do NOT trade 24 hours before/after major news (Fed, CPI, Earnings, etc.)
- Too much slippage risk, stops get gapped through
- Wait for dust to settle
