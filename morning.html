HIGH CONVICTION SETUPS

Source: SQLite bloodhound_scans/results from Bloodhound Scanner

Criteria: Score ≥ 56/80 AND (tradeable OR tier = HIGH_CONVICTION)

What it means: These are stocks at gamma walls (support/resistance) with multiple technical signals aligned. The score represents confluence across technicals, gamma levels, volume, and market context.

PRE-MARKET GAPS

Source: SQLite premarket_scans/movers from Pre-Market Scanner

Criteria: Gap ≥ 2% from previous close, tier ≠ FILTERED

Discovery Sources:

  • volume_leader - Top volume stocks from S&P 500 movers API
  • gainer - Stocks up 2%+ from yesterday's close
  • loser - Stocks down 2%+ from yesterday's close
  • nasdaq_mover - Top NASDAQ volume movers
  • watchlist - Your personal watchlist (data/watchlist.json)
  • core - Always checked: SPY, QQQ, IWM, DIA

How it works: The scanner queries /api/movers/$SPX and /api/movers/$COMPX to DYNAMICALLY discover what's moving across the entire market. This is NOT a fixed watchlist - it finds new opportunities each scan.

UNUSUAL OPTIONS FLOW

Source: SQLite opportunities from Opportunity Scanner

Criteria: Score ≥ 50 (only options-specific signals)

Signals tracked:

  • IV Rank - Where current IV sits vs. last year (high = expensive options)
  • Vol/OI Ratio - Today's volume vs. open interest (high = unusual activity)
  • C/P Ratio - Call volume vs. put volume (>1 = bullish bias)
  • Net Premium - Net $ flow into calls vs. puts

Note: Options don't trade pre-market (6-9:30 AM ET). This section only shows data during market hours.

EARNINGS THIS WEEK

Source: SQLite earnings_scans/results from Earnings Scanner

Criteria: Earnings date within next 10 days, sorted by date

What it means: Stocks with upcoming earnings events. IV typically spikes before earnings (premium selling opportunity) and crushes after (avoid holding through unless playing the move).

Days Days until earnings report. <3d = caution (IV crush imminent)
Time before_open (BMO) = pre-market gap risk. after_close (AMC) = overnight move risk.
IV Rank IV percentile. >70% = expensive premium (sell strategies favored)
Score PREM strategy score based on RSI + timing window. Higher = better pre-earnings momentum setup.

MARKET CONTEXT BAR

VIX Volatility index. <15 = complacent, 15-20 = normal, >20 = elevated/fear
ES/NQ SPY/QQQ % change from previous close. Shows market direction.
Bias Market direction based on index movement. BULLISH if avg change > 0.5%, BEARISH if < -0.5%.
VIX Regime complacent/normal/elevated/fear/capitulation - affects position sizing
Risk Risk appetite from Bloodhound scanner context

TIER MEANINGS

HIGH_CONVICTION Score ≥ 56 + at wall (0.5%), OR Score ≥ 64 + near wall (1.5%) - paper trade candidate
TRADEABLE Score ≥ 48 + at wall + trend-aligned - paper trade candidate
WATCH Interesting but not actionable yet - monitor for improvement

PM2 PROCESSES

pm2 list                    # Show all scanner status
pm2 logs bloodhound         # View Bloodhound scanner logs
pm2 logs premarket          # View Pre-Market scanner logs
pm2 logs opportunity        # View Opportunity scanner logs
pm2 restart all             # Restart all scanners
